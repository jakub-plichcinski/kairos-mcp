# Current naming directions

> The [2026-10-01 ranked research](RESEARCH-2026-10-01.md) supersedes the
> preliminary screening status below. WorkDefault is rejected; DefinedWay is a finalist.

Status: conceptual hypotheses as of 2026-10-01. **No finalist or recommendation;
none of these directions has current namespace clearance in this research.**
Evaluate against the [brief and north-star test](BRIEF.md) before promotion.
The earlier [candidate exploration](HISTORICAL-CANDIDATES.md) and
[Tacitloom recommendation](RECOMMENDATION.md) are historical, superseded work.

The territory is the gap between **generic and local**, **instinct and defined
procedure**, **possible and applicable**, **knows how and knows how here**,
and **model priors and institutional practice**. Useful vocabulary includes
local, rule, way, method, defined, practice, work, protocol, guide and path.
These are concepts for exploration, not a requirement to concatenate words.

| Direction | Why it fits “Create PR” | Strength | Concern to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RuleLane | Consult the rules defining how this task proceeds here. | Simple words; constraints without denying intelligence; fits guided execution. | Lane is metaphorical; persistent knowledge is not apparent. |
| DefinedWay | Use the way already defined for this task. | Direct philosophical fit; simple language; broader than AI, MCP or DevOps. | Broad; may sound like a methodology rather than software. |
| WorkDefault | Replace generic working defaults with applicable local ones. | Captures the pretrained/local distinction using familiar technical vocabulary. | Mandatory procedures are stronger than defaults; other meanings of default. |
| HouseRule | Check the rules that apply here despite knowing the general game. | Memorable analogy for why expertise alone is insufficient. | Informal; broad non-software usage creates a screening concern, not a verified collision. |
| LocalRule | Apply this user or team's rules to general competence. | Clear local-over-generic meaning within applicable authority. | Local may mean localhost; generic; perhaps better terminology than brand. |
| TeamMethod | Retrieve the team's chosen method before acting. | Simple; fits institutional knowledge. | Underrepresents individual users; generic and potentially hard to own. |
| MethodLane | A selected procedure establishes how execution proceeds. | Connects stored method with constrained execution; broader than routing. | Partly metaphorical; weaker identity and persistence signal. |

## Directions not advanced

| Direction | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Tacitloom | Superseded: obscure vocabulary and an explanatory metaphor conflict with international accessibility. Historical registry results do not repair that semantic weakness. |
| ActionRoute | Describes initial dispatch only; generic implementation vocabulary. |
| IntentRoute | Overemphasizes discovery; the discussion also reported a related AI routing collision. |
| RunbookMesh | Understates executable contracts; mesh suggests a topology not established by this brief. The discussion reported a related MCP use. |
| TaskMemory | Sounds like task history or vector memory and misses procedural enforcement. |
| ExactFlow | Understates persistent local knowledge; the discussion reported an AI/software brand collision. |
| ProtocolPath | Strong implementation fit but generic technical terminology; the discussion reported an existing OpenFF Evaluator use. Not advanced. |

The discussion also raised WorkRail, TeamSpec and RulePath as landscape
warnings, with a preference to avoid rail-based names. Those collision reports
are retained as **leads for verification**, not independently verified current
findings. Do not claim their availability or market status from this document.
