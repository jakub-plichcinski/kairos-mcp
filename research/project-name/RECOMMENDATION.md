# Recommendation: Tacitloom

**Tacitloom** (TASS-it-loom): **reusable know-how, carried through to action.**

The name joins tacit know-how with a loom: individual pieces of knowledge become an ordered, reusable whole. That fits the project's persistent procedures, semantic discovery, guided execution, and completion checks. It leaves room for humans and agents, different models, and interfaces beyond MCP.

The trade-off: “tacit” is less familiar than “task” or “step,” so introduce the name with the descriptor **procedural memory and guided execution for AI agents**.

## Decision and limits

**Recommend Tacitloom as the leading candidate.** Public checks show a coherent namespace with no discovered exact-name software collision. This is a researched recommendation, not a claim that every resource is secured.

Checked **2026-09-09 UTC**. A registry “not found” result is evidence of no record, not a guarantee that a provider will accept registration. Nothing has been registered or purchased. The optional `.io`, registrar pricing/reservations, final account acceptance, and formal trademark clearance remain unverified.

The MCP Registry [documents GitHub and domain-based namespace ownership](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/registry/authentication). A GitHub team is not the globally unique resource to secure; the organization is.

## Why this candidate

- **Praxory / Knowloom:** reject existing products in closely related AI/knowledge work: [Praxory](https://praxory.app/) and [KnowLoom](https://github.com/wang09904/KnowLoom).
- **Praxloom, Runweft, Praxelum, Knowferry:** exact `.com` already registered.
- **Skillweft:** exact npm package already exists.
- **Praxelet / Enactloom:** registry checks are similarly promising, but Tacitloom has the clearest combined story of retained know-how and structured execution.
- **Knowtread / Methodweft:** less natural spoken branding; deeper checking was unnecessary after stronger candidates emerged.

Public exact-name searches across software, MCP/skills directories, and indexed trademark sites surfaced no Tacitloom product collision. Results for the separated words included unrelated newspaper OCR. Native trademark databases were not comprehensively cleared; social and marketplace publisher handles are unverified.
